Tribes of Sound

A cultural anthropology of popular music, 1950s–today

Seventy years of popular music read as a story about people rather than records. Each era gets its tribe, its rituals, and the moral panic that arrived with the new sound — the panic being the interesting part, since it's how a society admits something real is changing. Underneath runs a second thread: how we physically listen, from the family radio everyone sat around to the earbud fitted to one person alone.
